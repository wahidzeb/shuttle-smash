How to play

Swipe to hit

Your player runs to the shuttle by itself. When the shuttle comes to you, swipe up anywhere on the screen. You can swipe early and the shot goes off at the best moment.

Direction aims left or right. Length and speed set the power. Tap plays a smart auto shot.

Shot types

The shot you play depends on how high the shuttle is when you hit it:

• High shuttle + long, fast swipe = SMASH (or a jump smash)
• High shuttle + medium swipe = CLEAR, deep to the back
• High shuttle + short swipe = DROP, just over the net
• Mid height + long swipe = flat DRIVE
• Low shuttle + long swipe = LIFT. Low + short swipe = NET SHOT

Swipe right when the shuttle reaches your racket to get a PERFECT hit. It is more accurate and more powerful.

Power shots

Rallies, perfect hits and winners fill your power ring. When it is full, tap the glowing power button (bottom right). Your next shot becomes a power shot:

Rules

Singles with rally scoring: every rally wins a point. First to 21 (or 11/15) wins, but you must lead by 2. At 29-all the next point wins (30 cap). The winner of a rally serves next. Serve diagonally, from the right court when your score is even. A shuttle that lands out or hits the net loses the point. Leave shuttles that are going out!

Keyboard & mouse

Drag with the mouse to swipe. Space auto shot · J soft · K medium · L/↑ power · hold ←/→ to aim · E arm power · P/Esc pause. Joystick mode: WASD move.

Progress

Earn coins from every match. Spend them in the Shop on Speed, Power, Control and Stamina training, new rackets, outfits and power shots. Clear the World Tour to face the Shuttle King!
